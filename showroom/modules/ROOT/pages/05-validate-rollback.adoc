= 5. Validate and rollback

== What you will learn

* How independent validation controls disposition after an action.
* Why failed validation triggers rollback and authority demotion.
* Why emergency stop wins before any target attempt.

== Objective

Prove that failed validation causes rollback and demotion, and that the emergency stop wins before action.

== See: exercise validation failure and emergency stop

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
for scenario in validation_failure kill_switch; do
  curl -fsS -X POST http://qualification:8080/api/v1/loop/run \
    -H 'content-type: application/json' \
    -H "x-correlation-id: lab-${scenario}-1" \
    -H "idempotency-key: lab-${scenario}-1" \
    -d "{\"scenario\":\"${scenario}\",\"requestedStage\":\"bounded_action\",\"namespace\":\"agentic-ai-601-lab-user\",\"approvalToken\":\"approval-${scenario}-1\",\"simulation\":true}"
  echo
done
----

For `validation_failure`, verify `rollbackTriggered`, `rollbackPassed`, and `demotionTriggered` are true. For `kill_switch`, verify denial with `emergency_stop_active` and zero attempts.

Validation evidence is logically independent of the action request. A claimed action success cannot override a failed observation.

=== Review lens

* Validation uses an independent observation.
* Failed validation produces rollback.
* Rollback success is separate from action success.
* Failed validation demotes authority.
* Emergency stop is evaluated before action permission.
* Kill-switch denial records zero attempts.
* Evidence retains one correlation chain.
* Human review remains required after rollback.

=== Evidence to retain

* independent validation result;
* rollback trigger and result;
* demotion result;
* emergency-stop decision;
* target-attempt count;
* correlation ID;
* reviewer requirement;
* resulting authority state.

== Verify

For `validation_failure`, confirm rollback and demotion are true and that
action success does not erase the failed validation result.

For `kill_switch`, confirm the reason is `emergency_stop_active`, target count
and attempt count remain zero, and no approval can override the stop. Record
both outcomes in the learner evidence chain.

Confirm that rollback does not promote authority or clear the requirement for
human review.

== Learner checkpoint

Record validation state, rollback state, demotion state, emergency-stop reason,
and target attempts for both scenarios.

*Pass when:* rollback succeeds, authority is demoted, and the kill switch prevents action before any target is touched.

== Key takeaway

Closed-loop safety depends on independent validation, automatic rollback and
demotion, and an emergency stop that precedes all action logic. Claimed action
success never outranks observed failure.
