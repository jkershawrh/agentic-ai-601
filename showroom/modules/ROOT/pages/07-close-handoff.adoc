= 7. Close and handoff

== What you will learn

* How to package a truthful non-certified factory receipt.
* How to preserve blockers, ownership, and immutable provenance.
* How independent Launchpad gates convert a package into an orderable lab.

== Objective

Package an honest, non-certified factory receipt for independent Launchpad review.

== See: inspect the fail-closed handoff

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
python3 - <<'PY'
import yaml
d = yaml.safe_load(open('handoff/launchpad-handoff.yaml'))
a = d['factory_receipt']['authority']
print({k: a[k] for k in ('orderable', 'certified', 'promotion_eligible')})
PY
----

Confirm the handoff declares `orderable: false`, `certified: false`, `promotion_eligible: false`, and `execution_authority_enabled: false`. No target environment or Launchpad state was changed. Automated promotion is false.

The remaining blockers are genuine: 401 certification, 501 certification, target and release approval, class-specific thresholds, live one-seat proof, capacity and reclaim graduation, artifact verification, and human promotion approval.

=== Handoff inventory

* immutable source revision;
* digest-pinned presentation and qualification images;
* factory development and presentation evidence;
* zero-residue factory evidence;
* versioned authority, policy, action, and evidence contracts;
* standard validation matrix, claim registry, and benchmark rubric;
* explicit prerequisite and destination blockers;
* named independent promotion authority.

=== Evidence to retain

* immutable source revision;
* image digests;
* factory evidence hashes;
* prerequisite blockers;
* destination-certification state;
* cleanup statement;
* next owner;
* next gate.

== Verify

Confirm the receipt reports `orderable: false`, `certified: false`, and
`promotion_eligible: false`. Confirm that the source revisions and image
digests are immutable and that no secret value appears in the handoff.

Check that 401 and 501 certification, trusted destination rendering, artifact
review, one-seat and five-seat proof, reclaim with zero residue, and human
promotion remain explicit. A factory receipt cannot waive those gates.

Verify that the cleanup statement accurately says no destination state was
changed during this rehearsal journey.

== Learner checkpoint

Record the immutable revision, artifact digests, evidence package, unmet
prerequisites, cleanup state, next owner, and next gate.

*Pass when:* the final artifact states what was proven, what remains REHEARSAL, and which independent authority must act next.

== Key takeaway

A trustworthy handoff makes the package reviewable without making it
orderable. Launchpad certification and human authority must be earned from
destination evidence, not inherited from the source factory.
