== Story

Agentic AI 401 established that one workflow could be operated with visible
evidence, deterministic policy, and human review. Agentic AI 501 then tested
whether that contract remained intact through load, pressure, and recovery.
The remaining question is deliberately narrower and more consequential: what
evidence would a human require before allowing one bounded, reversible action?

You are the authority reviewer. You will follow a synthetic condition from
signal to decision, challenge a single-use approval, simulate one bounded
action, force validation failure and rollback, and inspect the final evidence
chain. The runtime can recommend; it cannot promote itself or reach an
external target. In this candidate, production execution authority is disabled.

== Show → Learn → Do → Prove

* *Show:* use the presentation and qualification workspace to follow one
  source-labeled governed loop.
* *Learn:* separate signal, deterministic policy, approval, action,
  independent validation, rollback, learning, and human promotion authority.
* *Do:* execute the rehearsal API calls, challenge replay and emergency-stop
  behavior, and verify the hash-linked evidence chain.
* *Prove:* package immutable sources, bounded scope, zero unauthorized action,
  disabled external authority, cleanup state, blockers, next owner, and human
  disposition without claiming destination certification.

You will construct and challenge one governed loop:

`Signal → Decision → Action → Validate → Learn`
